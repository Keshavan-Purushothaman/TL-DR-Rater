How rating works

One headline at a time. Answer from the headline alone: would you want this in your news?

  • Swipe up: Must see. You would be annoyed to miss it.
  • Swipe right: Yes. You would read it.
  • Swipe left: No. Not interested.
  • Swipe down: Seen this story. Same story as an earlier card, in different words.
  • Junk. Not news at all: a quiz, an ad, clickbait.
  • Skip. You cannot tell from the headline. Records no opinion.

Made a mistake? Undo takes back the last answer.
